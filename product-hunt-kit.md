## 简介（260 字符内）

PawBudget helps dog owners plan the real cost of owning a dog — from puppy to senior years. Free interactive cost calculator, science-based dog age calculator, and guides on senior dog care, arthritis, and food. No account required.

## 首条评论（Maker's Comment）

Hi everyone! 👋 I'm Jin, and I built PawBudget after realizing most people underestimate what a dog actually costs over its lifetime — it's often $15,000–$30,000+.

PawBudget has two free tools:
- **Dog Cost Calculator** — estimate lifetime costs by breed, size, and age, including food, vet care, grooming, and emergencies
- **Dog Age Calculator** — a science-based conversion (not the old "×7" myth), with life-stage guidance

Plus guides on senior dog care, arthritis, and choosing food for older dogs.

Everything is free, no sign-up. I'd love your feedback — especially if you own a senior dog! 🐕
