Senior care basics that apply to every dog

Vet visits go twice-yearly

Senior checkups typically add bloodwork ($100–$200), urinalysis, and blood-pressure checks to catch kidney, liver, and thyroid issues early — when they're cheapest to manage.

Food needs change

Slower metabolisms need fewer calories but still plenty of high-quality protein, plus joint-supporting nutrients. See our senior dog food guide.

Budget for the vet, not just the food

Healthcare is already ~30% of annual costs and runs about 50% higher for seniors. Our cost calculator applies the senior adjustment automatically.

A note on medical content: these guides are educational and based on veterinary sources, but they are not veterinary advice. Every dog's condition is different — always work with your own veterinarian on diagnosis and treatment.
